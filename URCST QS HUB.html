Core Year 2 Construction Management Concepts

1. Procurement & Tendering

Selection of procurement routes (Traditional, Design & Build, Management Contracting) based on client risk allocation and speed required.

2. Valuation & Interim Certificates

Monthly measurements on site to quantify work in progress, incorporating retention deductions (5%) and materials on site allowances.

3. Variation & Final Account

Valuing additions and omissions against original BoQ rates, Dayworks, and adjusting Prime Cost and Provisional sums.
